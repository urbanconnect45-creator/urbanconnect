Launch and Execution Plan

  1. Present the collaboration proposal to the manager of Anishok.
  2. Reactivate Supabase and begin creating the UrbanConnect product catalogue.
  3. Organise supermarket products, prices, barcodes, stock data, and images.
  4. Test the customer and seller experience using real supermarket information.
  5. Produce and place the promotional banner after receiving permission from Anishok.
  6. Register the Google Play developer account and prepare the first public release.
  7. Measure registrations, orders, seller participation, and customer feedback before expanding.

Your Position as an Investor

I do not see your contribution as ordinary financial assistance. I see you as a major early investor and an important part of UrbanConnect's foundation. Your support should be formally recognised through a shareholding position in the company.

The ownership percentage and investment terms will be agreed clearly and documented when the company is formally incorporated. I will maintain records of how every investment is used and provide progress updates against the launch plan.

Founder's Commitment

I understand that belief must be supported by consistent action. I cannot promise that building the business will be effortless, but I can promise discipline, transparency, persistence, and responsible use of the investment.

I have failed before, but I have also learned from those failures. This time I have a clearer product, a realistic launch strategy, and a potential supermarket partnership. I genuinely believe I can make UrbanConnect succeed, and I would be honoured to build it with you as one of its earliest and most important investors.

Daniel Kenneth, Founder
